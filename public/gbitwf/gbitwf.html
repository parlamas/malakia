Why were they invented?

By the Hellenistic period, spoken Greek pronunciation was changing.

The accent marks were introduced primarily:

To help non-native speakers

To preserve correct pronunciation of Classical Greek

To assist in the reading of poetry (meter depended on pitch accent)

These diacritics were originally used in scholarly editions — not in everyday writing.
